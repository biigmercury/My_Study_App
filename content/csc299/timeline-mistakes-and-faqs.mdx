## The SIWES Timeline

"Compiled directly from the portal's schedule of activities." These are the dates for the **2026/2027 cohort** as published in the guide. **Confirm them on your own dashboard** — schedules can shift. The statuses update against today's date.

<Timeline title="Schedule of activities">

```timeline
2026-03-12 .. 2026-04-24 | Portal Registration | Complete your registration and profile setup on the portal.
2026-07-06 .. 2026-11-13 | Request for Introduction | Apply for the letter on the portal and download the approved PDF.
2026-10-08 | Training Orientation | Attend the mandatory school orientation briefing.
2026-11-01 .. 2026-11-27 | Submission of Acceptance Letter | Upload your signed Acceptance Letter from the company.
2026-11-09 .. 2026-12-06 | Submission of Job Reporting Form | Submit the SCAF signed by your company supervisor after commencing work.
2026-11-16 .. 2026-11-27 | Request for Placement | Submit your official company placement details on the portal.
2026-11-16 .. 2026-12-04 | Request for Change of Placement | Apply on the portal only if you absolutely must switch companies.
2026-11-09 .. 2027-01-22 | Training Exercise & Portal Logging | Work at your company and type your daily/weekly logs into the portal.
2027-01-11 .. 2027-01-31 | Submission of Employer Evaluation Form | Have your industry supervisor grade your performance and submit the form.
2027-01-18 .. 2027-02-07 | Submission of Work Report (Portal) | Upload your final technical report to the portal.
2027-02-08 .. 2027-02-12 | Physical Submission of Work Report | Submit the hard copy of your report to the ITCC centre/department.
2027-02-22 .. 2027-04-02 | Seminar Presentation (Defence) | Defend your SIWES experience before the department panel.
```

</Timeline>

**Reading the schedule:**
- The **Request for Introduction** window is long (July to November). Use it to **contact companies first**, so that every one of your limited requests counts.
- **Several windows overlap in November**: acceptance letter, Job Reporting Form, Request for Placement. Set reminders for each one.
- **Training runs about ten and a half weeks** on this schedule (9 November 2026 to 22 January 2027). The **evaluation and report windows open before training ends** — start the report early.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

| # | Mistake | Why it hurts / what to do instead |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Requesting introduction letters before contacting companies** | With the request cap, every ignored application is a **burned slot**. |
| 2 | **Sending the school's letter alone** | Always pair it with **your own application letter and CV**. |
| 3 | **Uploading blurry or cropped documents** | A rejected upload near a deadline is a **self-inflicted emergency**. |
| 4 | **Ignoring portal deadlines** | The Training Schedule is published early for a reason. **Set reminders.** |
| 5 | **Batch-typing logbook entries at the end** | Weeks of backlog plus portal downtime is a bad combination. |
| 6 | **Writing vague logbook entries** | "Worked in the office" gives your report and your panel nothing. |
| 7 | **Writing the report in one day** | It shows, and the defence exposes it. |
| 8 | **Missing supervisor signatures and stamps** | **Collect them as you go** — chasing a busy supervisor after training ends is painful. |
| 9 | **Not checking the portal regularly** | Statuses change, letters get approved, announcements drop. **Check weekly.** |
| 10 | **Choosing a company with no relevant work** | Three months of photocopying leaves you **nothing to present**. |
| 11 | **Poor conduct at the placement** | Lateness and attitude go straight into your **Employer Evaluation Form**. |
| 12 | **Leaving slides until the night before the defence** | Build them from your report, then practise three times. |
| 13 | **Not keeping personal copies** | **Photograph or scan every signed document** before submitting it. |

## Frequently Asked Questions

<Reveal question="1. Can I change my company after starting?">
**Yes**, through the **Request for Change of Placement** window on the portal. It's meant for **genuine problems**, like a company that gives you no work or shuts down. Apply within the window and inform the SIWES coordinator.
</Reveal>

<Reveal question="2. Can I fill my logbook at the end instead of daily?">
Some students try. You'll forget details, the entries will read as generic, and any portal issue in the final week becomes a crisis. **Log daily, type regularly.**
</Reveal>

<Reveal question="3. What if my supervisor delays or refuses to sign my forms?">
1. **Ask politely and explain the school deadline.**
2. If it persists, **escalate respectfully within the company** and **inform your school-based coordinator early**.

Don't wait until the deadline has passed.
</Reveal>

<Reveal question="4. What if I lose my logbook?">
**Report immediately to your department and the SIWES unit.** This is also why you **photograph your entries as you go**, so you can reconstruct them.
</Reveal>

<Reveal question="5. What if I miss a portal deadline?">
**Contact the coordinator immediately and explain.** Some issues can be resolved, but nothing is guaranteed — hence the reminders.
</Reveal>

<Reveal question="6. What should my report contain?">
- Introduction;
- the organisation;
- work done;
- skills acquired;
- challenges;
- conclusion and recommendations.

See *Writing Your SIWES Report* for all nine sections.
</Reveal>

<Reveal question="7. Do I get paid during SIWES?">
Some companies pay a stipend; many don't. It's between you and the company. **Prioritise a place where you'll actually learn over a small stipend where you won't.**
</Reveal>

<Reveal question="8. Can I do SIWES outside Ibadan or in another state?">
**Generally yes**, if the company is legitimate and you can still meet all portal requirements, including signed forms. **Confirm any location rules with the SIWES unit first.**
</Reveal>

<Reveal question="9. Can I train at a company owned by a family member?">
**Usually yes**, if it's a **real organisation doing relevant work** and a supervisor there **genuinely oversees and signs** your documents. The experience still has to **survive a panel's questions**.
</Reveal>

<Reveal question="10. How many companies can I apply to?">
The portal typically **caps introduction-letter requests at 5**, which is why you **confirm interest before requesting**.
</Reveal>

<Reveal question="11. What if no company accepts me?">
- **Widen the net early:** smaller companies, startups, government offices, personal connections.
- **Ask your department and seniors** for leads.
- If the deadline is closing in, **tell the coordinator instead of staying silent**.
</Reveal>

<Reveal question="12. Is SIWES graded?">
**Yes.** Your **logbook, report, employer evaluation and defence** are all assessed, and SIWES carries credit as a compulsory requirement. "Treat it like a course, because it is one."
</Reveal>

<Reveal question="13. Can I do SIWES remotely?">
Some tech companies offer **remote or hybrid** roles. The catch is paperwork: **signed forms, stamps and the evaluation still have to happen**. **Confirm with both the company and the SIWES unit** before accepting.
</Reveal>

<Reveal question="14. What should I wear to the defence?">
**Corporate or smart formal wear.** You want the panel focused on your presentation, not your outfit.
</Reveal>

<Reveal question="15. What happens if I don't complete SIWES?">
It's a **compulsory graduation requirement**, so an incomplete SIWES **follows you until it's resolved**, possibly by **repeating the exercise**. Do it properly once.
</Reveal>

<Reveal question="16. Where do I ask questions this handbook doesn't answer?">
- your **department's SIWES coordinator**;
- the **ITCC office**;
- your **course rep**;
- **seniors** who recently finished theirs.
</Reveal>

<Takeaway>
- **Know the schedule:** registration → introduction letters (contact companies first) → orientation → acceptance, SCAF and placement (all in November) → training and logging → evaluation and report → hard copy → defence. **Confirm dates on your dashboard.**
- **Top mistakes:** letters before contact; the school's letter alone; blurry uploads; ignored deadlines; batch or vague logs; a one-day report; missing signatures; irrelevant companies; poor conduct; last-minute slides; no personal copies.
- **When stuck:** your department's SIWES coordinator, the ITCC office, your course rep, recent seniors — and **speak up early**.
</Takeaway>

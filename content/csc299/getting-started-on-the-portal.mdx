## Step 1: Register and Set Up Your Profile

1. **Create your account** on the portal.
2. **Fill in your personal details** carefully.
3. Confirm that your **current level** and **current department** are correct.
4. **Double-check** your **matric number, email and phone number**.

> ⚠️ **Why the profile matters so much.** Your official letters are **generated from your profile**. "A wrong department on your profile means a wrong department on your letters." This matters even more **if you recently transferred departments**, as the guide's author did (Physics → Computer Science).

> 💡 **Tip.** Use a **professional-looking email address you actually check**. Companies may reply to the email on your documents, and "cutiepie4life@gmail.com" is not the first impression you want.

## Step 2: Study Your Training Schedule

After registering, you'll see your **Training Schedule** on your dashboard. It **dictates every deadline** in your journey: letters, forms, training dates and your defence.

> ⚠️ **Important.** "Portal deadlines are **not suggestions**. Missing one can delay your entire SIWES." **Screenshot your Training Schedule** and **set phone reminders a week before each deadline**.

Here is the schedule the guide compiled from the portal for the **2026/2027 cohort**, with live status against today's date. **Confirm the dates on your own dashboard** — schedules can shift.

### The portal's activities and statuses

The guide and its screenshots mention these portal activities. Learn the names now — you'll meet each one in a later lesson.

| Portal activity | What you do there | Phase |
|---|---|---|
| **Request for Introduction** (with **Browse Companies**) | Request your Introduction Letter for a company; download it under **View Letter** once approved | Before |
| **Submission of Acceptance Letter** (Acceptance / Undertaking Letter) | Upload the company's acceptance letter | Before |
| **Request for Placement** | Submit your official company details: company, supervisor, start and end dates | Before |
| **Request for Change of Placement** | Apply to switch companies — for genuine problems only | Before / early training |
| **Job Reporting Form** | Submit the signed and stamped SCAF | During (first two weeks) |
| **Training Logs** (daily and weekly logs) | Type your logbook entries day by day | During |
| **Submission of Work Report** | Upload your technical report | After |
| **Employer Evaluation Form** | Your supervisor's grading (uploaded or submitted physically, as instructed) | After |

**Statuses you'll see:**
- **SUBMITTED** — waiting for the coordinator.
- **APPROVED** — ready, for example to download your letter.
- **ACTIVE** — your placement is running.
- **"Submission Period Closed"** — the window has passed. **Statuses change and announcements drop, so check the portal weekly.**

<SortingGame title="Before, during or after training?">


<Takeaway>
- Everything runs through the **ITCC portal**: letters, submissions, logs, deadlines.
- **Profile:** correct **level, department, matric number, email and phone** — your letters are generated from it. Use a **professional email** you actually check.
- **Training Schedule:** it sets every deadline. **Screenshot it**, set **reminders a week ahead**, and check the portal **weekly**.
- **Statuses:** SUBMITTED → APPROVED; ACTIVE placement; "Submission Period Closed" means you missed the window.
</Takeaway>

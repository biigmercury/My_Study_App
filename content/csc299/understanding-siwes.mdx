## What Is SIWES?

**SIWES** is the **Students' Industrial Work Experience Scheme**: a **national programme run through the Industrial Training Fund (ITF)** in partnership with universities and employers across Nigeria.

The idea is simple: **classroom knowledge alone isn't enough**. Students spend a period **working in a real organisation** to gain hands-on experience in their field. UI's Industrial Training Coordinating Centre puts it in its motto: *"Bridging the gap between theory and practice."*

**At UI:**
- The scheme is coordinated by the school's industrial training unit, the **ITCC** (Industrial Training Coordinating Centre).
- It is managed on an **online portal** that handles everything from registration to your final report.
- On the portal your course appears as **CSC 299 — Industrial Training I (3 credit units)**, with a **training duration of 3 months**.

### Why it matters

SIWES is **compulsory and assessed**, but it's also genuinely valuable. Done well, it gives you:
- **real work experience** for your CV;
- **exposure** to how your field works outside the classroom;
- **professional contacts** who can recommend you later;
- **a clearer sense of what you actually enjoy doing**.

It is also a **graduation requirement**. Your **logbook, report, employer evaluation and defence** are all assessed, and SIWES carries credit units. "Treat it like a course, because it is one." An incomplete SIWES "follows you until it's resolved, possibly by repeating the exercise. **Do it properly once.**"

## Background *(beyond the guide)*

- The **Industrial Training Fund (ITF)** was established by the Federal Government in **1971**. It started SIWES in **1973** because many graduates lacked practical skills for work in industry.
- The scheme is supervised for each type of institution by a regulator: the **National Universities Commission (NUC)** for universities, the **NBTE** for polytechnics and the **NCCE** for colleges of education. The ITF coordinates the whole.
- The scheme's commonly stated **objectives** are to:
  1. help students **acquire industrial skills and experience** in their course of study;
  2. **prepare students for the work situations** they will meet after graduation;
  3. **expose students to work methods and equipment** not available in their institutions;
  4. **make the transition from school to work easier** and build contacts for later job placement;
  5. let students **apply theoretical knowledge to real work** — bridging theory and practice;
  6. **involve employers** in the educational process.

## The Key Players

| Player | Role |
|---|---|
| **You** | Find a placement, do the work, keep records, submit on time. |
| **The school (ITCC / SIWES unit)** | Issues your letters, runs the portal, sets deadlines, assesses your submissions. Your **department's SIWES coordinator** is your first contact for problems. |
| **Your employer** | The company that trains you. Your **industry-based supervisor** there signs your forms and **grades your performance**. |
| **The ITF** | The **national body** behind the scheme. |

<SortingGame title="Who does it?">

```sort
@buckets You | ITCC / SIWES unit | Employer (industry supervisor) | ITF
Finds a company willing to take a student => You :: Placement hunting is your job — start with people.
Issues and approves the Introduction Letter on the portal => ITCC / SIWES unit :: The coordinator processes your request.
Signs and stamps the Job Reporting Form (SCAF) => Employer (industry supervisor) :: The stamp proves you have physically started work.
Sets the deadlines on the Training Schedule => ITCC / SIWES unit :: The schedule appears on your portal dashboard.
Fills the logbook every working day => You :: Then types the entries into the portal.
Grades your punctuality, attitude and output => Employer (industry supervisor) :: Through the Employer Evaluation Form.
Is the national body behind the scheme => ITF :: The Industrial Training Fund.
Assesses your report and runs the defence => ITCC / SIWES unit :: With your department's panel of lecturers.
```

## The Journey at a Glance

"SIWES looks intimidating, but it's really **a series of small, manageable tasks**." The journey has three phases:

<FlowDiagram title="Before → During → After">
<FlowStep title="1. Before training">
- **Register on the portal** and check your profile.
- **Find willing companies** (people first, portal second).
- **Request an Introduction Letter.**
- **Apply** with a complete package.
- **Upload the Acceptance Letter** and complete the **Request for Placement**.
</FlowStep>
<FlowStep title="2. During training">
- Submit the **Job Reporting Form (SCAF)** within the first two weeks.
- **Work**, and **log your activities daily** — in the physical logbook and on the portal.
</FlowStep>
<FlowStep title="3. After training">
- Submit your **technical report** (portal and hard copy) and the **Employer Evaluation Form**.
- **Defend your experience** before a departmental panel.
</FlowStep>
</FlowDiagram>

<OrderPuzzle title="Put the SIWES journey in order">

```order
Register on the portal and check your profile :: Chapter 2 — the profile generates your letters.
Find companies that will take you :: Chapter 3, Step 1 — people first.
Request the Introduction Letter on the portal :: Only for companies that said yes (cap of 5).
Send your application package :: Cover letter + Introduction Letter + CV.
Upload the Acceptance Letter and complete the Request for Placement :: Two separate portal tasks.
Submit the Job Reporting Form (SCAF) :: Within the first two weeks of training.
Log your work daily (physical logbook and portal) :: Throughout training.
Submit the technical report and the Employer Evaluation Form :: Near the end of training.
Defend your experience before the panel :: The seminar presentation.
```

</OrderPuzzle>

<Takeaway>
- **SIWES** = Students' Industrial Work Experience Scheme, run through the **ITF** (est. 1971; SIWES since 1973). At UI it is coordinated by the **ITCC** on an online portal — **CSC 299, Industrial Training I, 3 units, 3 months**.
- **Players:** **you** (placement, work, records, deadlines), the **ITCC/SIWES unit** (letters, portal, deadlines, assessment), your **employer** (supervisor signs forms and grades you), the **ITF** (national body).
- **Journey:**
  - *before* — portal, introduction letter, application, acceptance and placement;
  - *during* — SCAF, daily logging;
  - *after* — report, evaluation, defence.
- It is **compulsory and graded**: logbook, report, employer evaluation and defence. Do it properly once.
</Takeaway>

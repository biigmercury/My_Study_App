"You can't start training without a placement, and you can't secure one without the right letters." Here is the **exact order of operations**.

<OrderPuzzle title="The five steps, in order">

```order
Find willing companies first :: Start with people, not the portal.
Request your Introduction Letter :: Only for companies that said yes — the portal caps requests at about 5.
Send a complete application :: Cover letter + Introduction Letter + CV — never the school's letter alone.
Collect and upload the Acceptance Letter :: The same day you receive it.
Complete the Request for Placement :: A separate portal task — do both.
```

</OrderPuzzle>

## Step 1: Find Willing Companies First

**"Don't start with the portal. Start with people."**
- Ask **seniors** where they did their SIWES.
- Ask **family, friends and neighbours** who work in relevant organisations.
- **Search** for companies in your field, in Ibadan or wherever you plan to train.
- **Contact them directly:** *"Do you accept SIWES students, and would you take me for this period?"*

**Only move to the portal once a company has said yes, or shown strong interest.**

> ⚠️ **Important.** The portal allows only a **limited number of Introduction Letter requests — typically capped at 5**. "Every request sent to a company that ignores you is a **wasted slot**. Confirm interest first, then request the letter."

**Choose relevant work.** "Three months of photocopying leaves you nothing to present." For Computer Science, look at:
- software and web companies;
- IT departments of banks, hospitals and government offices;
- networking and ISP firms;
- data and analytics teams;
- computer-repair and support centres;
- tech hubs.

---

## Step 2: Request Your Introduction Letter

The **Introduction Letter** is a formal letter **from the school** confirming to an employer that you're **a real student who needs a placement**.

1. On the portal, open the **Request for Introduction** activity.
2. Use **Browse Companies**, or type in your target company.
3. **Submit.** The status will show **SUBMITTED**.
4. **Wait a few days** for the coordinator to process it.
5. **Download the approved PDF** under **View Letter**.

<Checklist title="Before requesting an Introduction Letter">

```check
The company has confirmed they take SIWES students
Someone there is expecting your application
You have the company's correct name and address
Your CV is ready (optional)
Your application letter is drafted (see Documents You Need)
```

</Checklist>

## Step 3: Send a Complete Application

**"Never send the school's letter alone."** Your application package should contain:

1. **Your application (cover) letter** — see *Documents You Need* for the template and a builder;
2. **the official Introduction Letter** from the portal;
3. **your CV** (optional, but it helps).

**Deliver it however the company prefers.** "Some want email, some want a printed copy at the front desk. **When in doubt, do both.**"

---

## Step 4: Collect and Submit Your Acceptance Letter

If the company accepts you, it will issue an official **Acceptance Letter**, usually **on its letterhead**. Once you have it:

1. **Scan it, or take a clean, well-lit photo.** No shadows; letterhead fully visible.
2. On the portal, go to **Submission of Acceptance Letter** and **upload it**.

> 💡 **Mercury's tip.** "**Upload your Acceptance Letter the same day you receive it.** Companies can take weeks to issue letters. Don't stack their delay on top of yours."

<DocAnatomy title="What a company's Acceptance Letter contains (simplified)">

```doc
@part head = Company letterhead :: Logo, registration number (RC), address, phone and email. Without a letterhead the letter may not be accepted.
@part to = Addressed to the ITCC :: The Assistant Director, Industrial Training Coordinating Centre (ITCC), University of Ibadan.
@part subj = Subject :: Acceptance of industrial training placement for [your name] (Matric No. …).
@part ref = Reference to your Introduction Letter :: It refers to the school's letter by its date.
@part accept = The acceptance :: Confirms your name, level, department, the 3-month duration and the company.
@part commit = Commitment to supervise :: The company promises relevant, hands-on experience and adequate supervision.
@part sign = Signature, name, title and stamp :: Signed by an officer of the company (CEO, HR or technical manager) and stamped with the date.
head^: **[COMPANY LOGO] · [COMPANY NAME]** · RC [number]
head>: [Head office address] · [Phone] · [Email]

to: The Assistant Director,
to: Industrial Training Coordinating Centre (ITCC),
to: University of Ibadan, Ibadan.

to: Dear Sir,

subj^: **ACCEPTANCE OF INDUSTRIAL TRAINING PLACEMENT FOR [STUDENT'S NAME] (MATRIC NO. [123456])**

ref: We refer to your official Introduction Letter dated [date], regarding the placement of your student, [Name], for the Students' Industrial Work Experience Scheme (SIWES).

accept: We are pleased to inform you that we have accepted [Name], a [200]-level student from your Department of [Department], for the required 3-month industrial training placement at [Company].

commit: We confirm our commitment to providing the student with practical, hands-on experience relevant to their field of study, and we will ensure they are adequately supervised throughout the training period.

sign: Yours faithfully,
sign: [Signature]                      [Company stamp, signed and dated]
sign: [Name], [CEO / Technical Manager]
```

</DocAnatomy>

## Step 5: Complete the Request for Placement

The portal has a **separate Request for Placement** activity, where you submit your **official company details**: company, company supervisor, start date and end date. When that window opens (check the timeline), fill it in.

> **Uploading the acceptance letter and completing this request are two different tasks. Do both.**

Once both are approved, your placement shows as **ACTIVE**. A **Request for Change of Placement** window also exists, but it is for **genuine problems only** — a company that gives you no work, or shuts down. Apply within the window and inform the SIWES coordinator.

<Takeaway>
- **Order:** find willing companies → **Request for Introduction** → send the **full package** → upload the **Acceptance Letter** → complete the **Request for Placement**.
- **People first:** seniors, family, friends, direct contact — "Do you accept SIWES students?" Choose **relevant** work.
- **Introduction-letter requests are capped (about 5)** — request only for companies that said yes.
- **Never send the school's letter alone:** cover letter + Introduction Letter + CV; email and/or hard copy.
- **Acceptance Letter:** on letterhead, scanned cleanly, uploaded **the same day**.
- **Change of Placement** is for genuine problems only, within its window.
</Takeaway>
